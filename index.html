🇷🇺 SOVIET STYLE — COMPLETE A TO Z
Origin: Developed in Soviet sport institutes from the 1930s. Scientific, systematic, volume-based. Produced Lomachenko, Golovkin, Usyk, both Klitschkos. The most technically complete boxing system ever created.
STANCE & GUARD ▼
Feet Position:
• Lead foot (left) at 45° pointing toward opponent
• Rear foot perpendicular, shoulder-width apart
• Weight: 60% rear / 40% front
• Knees slightly bent — never locked, never deep
• Stay on balls of feet — heels barely touch

Guard — Asymmetric (KEY difference):
• Lead hand extended 60–70% — it threatens AND defends
• Rear hand at chin level, elbow protecting ribs
• Chin tucked into chest — always
• Shoulders slightly rolled forward
• Elbows pointing DOWN — protect liver and ribs

Not a shell: The extended lead hand is a weapon. You control range with it. This is the Soviet jab threat that forces opponents to respect distance.
THE SOVIET JAB — YOUR MAIN WEAPON ▼
The jab is not a setup in Soviet style — it IS the weapon. Lomachenko and Usyk win rounds with jabs alone.

Mechanics:
• Rotate shoulder forward as you extend — adds 4–6 inches reach
• Step with lead foot FIRST, then jab follows — never jab then step
• Snap wrist at impact — palm faces DOWN at contact
• Return along EXACT same path — no loops
• Rear hand does NOT move during jab — stays at chin

4 Types — Master All:
1. Stiff Jab — Fast, no weight. Measures distance, controls space
2. Power Jab — Full shoulder rotation + step. Meant to damage
3. Double Jab — First breaks guard, second hits exposed face
4. Body Jab — Drop level, jab to solar plexus. Forces guard down for cross

Daily drill: 100 jabs in mirror every morning. Count them. Make every single one identical and perfect.
FOOTWORK — RING GENERAL ▼
Soviet footwork is about CONTROL — you move the opponent, they don't move you.

Core Movements:
• Step & Jab: Lead foot steps → jab follows. Always this order.
• Pivot: On lead foot, rotate 45–90°. Creates new angle after every combo. Lomachenko's signature.
• Lateral exit: After landing, step OUT sideways — never straight back. Straight back = cornered.
• Circle away from power hand: Move left to avoid right cross. Circle right to set up your hook.

Tape Drill (5 min daily):
4 corners, 1m square of tape. Forward 4 steps → pivot → right 4 → pivot → back → pivot → left → pivot. Then random for 3 min.

Golden rule: After every single combination — MOVE. Never punch and stand still. That is when you get counter-hit.
CROSS & SIGNATURE COMBINATIONS ▼
Cross Mechanics:
• Rear heel rotation — power begins here, not the arm
• Hip rotates toward target — 60% of all power
• Shoulder follows hip — not forced, natural
• Full extension, wrist snaps at contact
• Return by rotating back — don't pull arm

Soviet Signature Combinations:
1. Soviet Basic: Jab → Cross → Pivot out
2. Pressure: Double Jab → Cross → Lateral exit
3. Level Change: Jab (head) → Cross (body) → Hook (head)
4. Setup: Jab → Jab → Cross → Lead Hook → Pivot
5. Counter: Step back (bait miss) → Jab → Cross
6. Advanced: Jab → slip outside → cross → 90° pivot → jab

Rule: Every combination ends with movement. Punch → move → reassess. No exceptions.
SOVIET DEFENSE SYSTEM ▼
Soviet defense is ACTIVE — you're setting up your counter while defending.

Slipping:
• Move head OUTSIDE punch line — small, precise (3–5 inches only)
• Slip outside (right) from their jab → left hook counter position
• Slip inside (left) from their jab → straight right counter
• The slip is a WEIGHT SHIFT — not a lean. Feet stay planted.

Parry + Counter (Classic Soviet):
• Lead hand redirects their jab to your right
• Immediately counter with rear cross — this IS the Soviet 1-2 counter

Footwork as Defense:
• Most punches miss because you weren't there
• Step back → pivot → circle → make them miss BY MOVING

Lomachenko's secret: Until age 11, no boxing — only Ukrainian Hopak folk dance. His footwork is the direct result. Obsessive repetition of ONE skill creates mastery.
SOVIET TRAINING METHODOLOGY ▼
Volume before speed: Learn it slow and correct. Speed is the result of 10,000 perfect reps. You cannot rush this.

Soviet Daily Minimums:
• 100 jabs in mirror (every morning)
• 5 min footwork pattern drilling
• 3 shadowboxing rounds with single-skill focus
• 3 heavy bag rounds (technique not power)
• Specific skill rounds: "this round only slipping"

Progression by month:
Month 1–2: Jab, stance, footwork only
Month 3–4: Cross, defense, 2-punch combinations
Month 5–6: Hooks, uppercuts, body work
Month 7+: Sparring, counters, ring generalship
👊 PEEKABOO STYLE — COMPLETE A TO Z
Origin: Created by Cus D'Amato in the 1950s. Built for shorter fighters against taller opponents. Floyd Patterson, Jose Torres, Mike Tyson. The most aggressive, psychologically devastating style in boxing history. Goal: impossible to hit cleanly + impossible to stop.
PEEKABOO STANCE & GUARD ▼
Opposite of Soviet in almost every way:

Feet:
• Much more square — chest faces opponent at ~45° max
• Slightly wider than shoulder width
• Weight 50/50 — ready to explode in all directions
• Deeper crouch — knees more bent than normal
• Lowered head height IS your defense

The Peekaboo Guard:
• Both fists at CHEEKBONES — peek at opponent through fists
• Elbows tight together, pointing straight down
• Forearms parallel and close — create a complete wall
• Chin tucked DOWN into chest
• BOTH shoulders protect chin simultaneously
• Head NEVER still — constant small movement at all times

Why it's impenetrable: Chin below shoulder level + hands at cheeks = no clean target. Even if they land, they hit arms or take glancing blows off a moving head. The guard does the defending so you can attack.
HEAD MOVEMENT — THE SOUL OF PEEKABOO ▼
This is the hardest part. It must become involuntary — like breathing.

Bob & Weave:
• Bend knees → drop head to one side → rise on the OTHER side
• Draw a U-shape with your head. Down, across, up.
• DOWN-UNDER-UP — not forward, not backward
• Always come up on the OUTSIDE of their punch — counter position
• Rule: Every rise = a punch. No free passes.

Slip:
• Pure weight shift — 4–5 inch head movement off center line
• Slip their jab outside (right) → right hand counter
• Slip inside (left) → left hook body → left hook head

The Pull Counter (Tyson's KO weapon):
• Stand at jab range — bait the jab
• As jab comes: shift weight to rear foot — jab misses past face
• IMMEDIATELY explode forward with rear hand counter
• This is pure timing. Practice 50x per session until it's reflex.

Drill: Hang a string at nose height. Bob under it 100 times. Every time you rise — throw a hook. Do this every session.
INFIGHTING & BODY ATTACK ▼
Getting Inside Safely:
• Don't walk in — SLIP IN. Bob under jab, rise inside their range
• Once inside: tight elbows protect your body
• Push off their arms slightly to create space for hooks
• Exit by stepping to an angle — never straight back

Body-Head System (Tyson's trademark):
The body is the tree. Chop the tree → branches fall → head exposed.

1. Left hook body → Right uppercut chin
2. Right hook liver → Left hook temple
3. Left body → Right body → Left hook head (Tyson's pattern)
4. Double left hook: body then head in one fluid motion

The Liver Shot:
• Left hook to RIGHT side of their torso (under right elbow)
• Even a light liver shot causes involuntary muscle drop
• Dig UNDER the elbow — it's a digging hook, not a swing
• Setup: jab to head → guard rises → left hook to body
PEEKABOO COMBINATIONS A–Z ▼
Level 1 — Foundation (Month 1–2):
1. Left hook body → Left hook head
2. Right hook body → Left hook head
3. Left hook → Right cross
4. Slip right → Right cross counter

Level 2 — Integration (Month 3–4):
5. Bob left → come up with left hook → right uppercut
6. Slip outside → right hand → roll under → left hook
7. Double left hook (body-head) → right cross
8. Pull back counter → right hand → left hook

Level 3 — Advanced (Month 5+):
9. Jab (Soviet range) → slip in (Peekaboo) → left hook body → right uppercut
10. Slip right → cross → bob under counter → left hook
11. Right hook body → left hook body → right uppercut
12. HYBRID: Jab (Soviet) → slip inside → body combo → pivot exit (Soviet)

Learning order: Shadow slow ×50 → Shadow full speed ×50 → Heavy bag ×30 → Mitts → Sparring. Never skip steps.
PEEKABOO FOOTWORK — CUT OFFS ▼
Cut the Ring — Don't Chase:
• Step DIAGONALLY to block their escape route
• They go right → you step diagonally right (not straight right)
• Herd them to corners and ropes — funnel the target

The Step-Step-Punch (Tyson's signature):
• Two fast steps to close distance → punch lands with step 2
• One moment at range, next moment inside — opponent can't process

The fundamental difference from Soviet: Soviet footwork creates and controls distance — you're the sniper. Peekaboo footwork eliminates distance — you're the missile. Both live in you.
THE PEEKABOO MENTALITY (Cus D'Amato) ▼
"The hero and the coward both feel the same fear. The hero uses it. The coward runs from it." — Cus D'Amato

Peekaboo Mental Rules:
• You are always the AGGRESSOR — you dictate, never just react
• Every miss by opponent is YOUR opportunity — punish every miss
• Get hit? Counter IMMEDIATELY. Never absorb without responding.
• Head is always moving. Stillness = target. Motion = invisible.
• Pressure is your weapon — relentless forward intent breaks minds before bodies

Pre-session visualization (2 min, every time):
Eyes closed. See yourself slipping cleanly. See the counter land perfectly. Feel the timing. Your brain cannot distinguish between a vivid visualization and a real rep. This is real training.

💡 DAILY CODING PROTOCOL
25-10-25 method: 25 min review yesterday's patterns. 10 min plan today's approach on PAPER first (no code). 25 min code + debug. 15 min read optimal solution and understand the gap. 15 min second similar problem.
Pattern recognition > memorization. There are ~30 patterns in DSA. Once you know all 30, every new problem is a known combination. Learn patterns — not individual problems.
Stuck protocol: 15 min brute force. 15 more min for better approach. 30 min total stuck → hint only. 45 min → look at solution. Close it, open blank file, recode from memory.
NEVER copy-paste. Even when you look at the answer — close it, open a blank file, type from memory. If you can't, you don't know it. This rule is non-negotiable.

JAVA STUDY TIPS
OOP interview trap: Don't define polymorphism — demonstrate it. "Here's a real example I coded..." Interviewers test if you USED it, not if you read about it.

🧠 MENTAL PERFORMANCE
5-second rule: When you don't feel like doing something — 5-4-3-2-1 and move. Your brain rationalizes laziness in under 5 seconds. Don't give it the chance.
Identity-first habits: Don't say "I'm trying to code." Say "I'm a developer." Don't say "I'm learning boxing." Say "I'm a fighter." Your actions follow your identity, not your motivation.
Process over outcome: You can't control if you get the job. You can control whether you coded for 1.5 hours today. Focus on what you control. The outcome follows the process — always.
Visualization (nightly, 3 min): See your future self in detail. How do you walk when you have the job? What's your energy like? This isn't woo — it's neuroscience. The brain cannot distinguish between vivid imagination and reality.
Comparison trap: Someone else placing is not your loss. Their success is proof it's possible. Use it as fuel, not poison.
💤 RECOVERY IS TRAINING
Sleep is non-negotiable. 7.5 hours minimum. Muscle grows during sleep. DSA solutions arrive during sleep. Willpower, decision quality, mood — all depend on sleep. Train hard, sleep harder.
90-min sleep cycles: Set alarm at 6hr or 7.5hr or 9hr from when you sleep. Waking mid-cycle causes grogginess. At cycle end — natural.
Pre-interview nerves: Anxiety before something important is your body preparing you — elevated heart rate, focus, adrenaline. Reframe: "I'm excited" not "I'm scared." Same physiology, completely different performance.
Cold water on face: Triggers dive reflex — immediately lowers heart rate. Use before coding tests, interviews, any high-pressure moment.
Creatine: 3–5g daily consistently. Proven to improve physical AND cognitive performance. Best per-rupee investment for what you're building.
⚡ LIFE SYSTEMS
Time blocking beats to-do lists. Schedule blocks: 8–9:30am deep coding. 4–5:30pm gym. 6–7:30pm boxing. 9pm reading. Lists get ignored. Blocks in a schedule get done.
Environment design: Workout clothes laid out the night before. Books on the pillow. Phone charger in another room. You follow the path of least resistance — design the right path.
2-minute rule: If it takes under 2 minutes — do it now. Tracking and avoiding small tasks costs more mental energy than doing them.
Job search as a system: Track every application: Company, Role, Date, Status, Contact, Follow-up date. Searching without tracking is boxing without footwork — you'll exhaust before landing anything.
Protect mornings. No social media, no news, no messages for first 90 minutes after waking. Your brain is most powerful then. Don't waste it on other people's agendas.
📱 PHONE DISCIPLINE
Phone-free zones: Meals, gym, boxing, first and last 30 min of day. Non-negotiable. Partial presence is worse than absence.
Before picking up: Ask "Why am I picking this up?" No clear answer → don't pick it up. This one habit alone gives back 2+ hours per day.
No phone 1 hour before sleep. Blue light suppresses melatonin. Sleep quality directly determines next-day performance in coding, boxing, everything.
Grayscale mode: Switch phone display to grayscale. Color is designed to be stimulating. Grayscale makes the phone boring. Boring phone = more life.
Apps to delete: Every app that you open when bored without intention. Replace with a habit that serves your goals.
