🇷🇺 SOVIET STYLE — COMPLETE A TO Z
Origin: Developed in Soviet sport institutes 1930s–1990s. Scientific, systematic, volume-based. Produced Lomachenko, Golovkin, Usyk, both Klitschkos. Most technically complete system ever created.
STANCE & GUARD ▼
Feet: Lead foot 45° toward opponent · Rear foot perpendicular · Shoulder-width apart · Weight 60% rear / 40% front · Balls of feet — heels barely touch

Guard (Asymmetric — KEY): Lead hand extended 60–70% — threatens AND defends · Rear hand at chin, elbow protecting ribs · Chin tucked into chest — always · Shoulders rolled forward slightly · Elbows pointing DOWN

Critical: Soviet guard is NOT a shell. The extended lead hand is a weapon that controls range. This is what forces opponents to respect distance.
THE SOVIET JAB — YOUR MAIN WEAPON ▼
The jab is not a setup in Soviet style — it IS the weapon. Lomachenko wins rounds with jabs alone.

Mechanics: Rotate shoulder forward (adds 4–6" reach) · Step lead foot FIRST then jab follows · Snap wrist at impact, palm DOWN · Return along EXACT same path · Rear hand NEVER moves

4 Types — Master All:
1. Stiff Jab — Fast, no weight. Measures distance.
2. Power Jab — Full shoulder rotation + step. Meant to damage.
3. Double Jab — First breaks guard, second hits open face.
4. Body Jab — Drop level, jab to solar plexus. Forces guard down for cross.

Daily minimum: 100 jabs in mirror every morning. Count them. Perfect every one.
FOOTWORK — RING GENERAL ▼
Soviet footwork is about CONTROL — you move the opponent, they don't move you.

Core Movements:
• Step & Jab: Lead foot steps → jab follows. This order. Always.
• Pivot: On lead foot, 45–90°. Creates new angle after every combo. Lomachenko's DNA.
• Lateral exit: Step sideways after landing — never straight back.
• Circle away from power hand: Move left to avoid right cross.

Tape Drill (5 min daily): 4 corners, 1m square. Forward → pivot → right → pivot → back → pivot → left → pivot. Then random.

Golden rule: After every single combination — MOVE. Standing still after punching is when you get counter-hit.
SOVIET DEFENSE SYSTEM ▼
Soviet defense is ACTIVE — you're setting up the counter while defending.

Slipping: Move head OUTSIDE punch line — 3–5 inches only · Slip outside (right) from their jab → left hook counter · Slip inside (left) → right cross counter · Weight shift only — feet stay planted

Parry + Counter (Classic Soviet): Lead hand redirects their jab right → immediately counter with cross. This IS the fundamental Soviet 1-2 counter.

Footwork as Defense: Most punches miss because you weren't there. Step back → pivot → circle → make them miss by moving.

Lomachenko's secret: No boxing until age 11 — only Ukrainian Hopak folk dance. His footwork is the direct result. Obsessive repetition of ONE skill creates mastery.
CROSS & SIGNATURE COMBINATIONS ▼
Cross Mechanics: Rear heel rotation first (power starts here) → hip rotates toward target (60% of power) → shoulder follows → full extension → wrist snaps at contact → return by rotating back.

Soviet Signature Combinations:
1. Soviet Basic: Jab → Cross → Pivot out
2. Pressure: Double Jab → Cross → Lateral exit
3. Level Change: Jab head → Cross body → Hook head
4. Setup: Jab → Jab → Cross → Lead Hook → Pivot
5. Counter: Step back (bait miss) → Jab → Cross
6. Advanced: Jab → slip outside → Cross → 90° pivot → Jab

Rule: Every combo ends with movement. Punch → move → reassess.
SOVIET TRAINING METHODOLOGY ▼
Volume before speed: Learn slow and correct. Speed is the result of 10,000 perfect reps. You cannot rush this process.

Soviet Daily Minimums: 100 jabs in mirror · 5 min footwork patterns · 3 shadow rounds with single-skill focus · 3 bag rounds (technique first) · Specific skill rounds

Progression by month:
Month 1–2: Jab, stance, footwork only — master before adding
Month 3–4: Cross, defense, 2-punch combinations
Month 5–6: Hooks, uppercuts, body work
Month 7+: Sparring, counters, ring generalship
HYBRID STYLE PHILOSOPHY ▼
The Goal: Use Soviet footwork + jab to control distance from outside. When opponent commits to close the gap — switch to Peekaboo, slip inside, destroy with body-head.

You are building: The fighter with no range weakness. Comfortable at long range (Soviet) and deadly at short range (Peekaboo). This is what makes you truly dangerous.

Tyson as model: Opponents couldn't figure out the distance — too close and Peekaboo destroyed them, too far and his explosive footwork closed the gap instantly. That is what you're building.
👊 PEEKABOO STYLE — COMPLETE A TO Z
Origin: Created by Cus D'Amato 1950s. Built for shorter fighters vs taller opponents. Floyd Patterson, Mike Tyson. Most aggressive, psychologically devastating style in history. Goal: impossible to hit cleanly + impossible to stop.
STANCE & GUARD ▼
Opposite of Soviet in almost every way:

Feet: Much more square — chest faces opponent at ~45° · Wider than shoulder width · Weight 50/50 · Deep crouch — knees significantly bent · Lower head height IS your defense

The Peekaboo Guard: Both fists at CHEEKBONES — you literally peek through fists · Elbows tight together, pointing down · Forearms parallel — complete wall · Chin tucked into chest · BOTH shoulders protect chin · Head NEVER still — constant small movement

Why it works: Chin below shoulder level + hands at cheeks = no clean target. Even if they land, they hit arms or glancing blows off a moving head.
HEAD MOVEMENT — THE SOUL OF PEEKABOO ▼
This must become involuntary — like breathing.

Bob & Weave: Bend knees → drop head to one side → rise on OTHER side · Draw a U with your head · DOWN-UNDER-UP · Always rise on OUTSIDE of punch → counter position · RULE: every rise = a punch

Slip: Pure weight shift — 4–5 inches off center line · Slip right from their jab → right hand counter · Slip left → left hook body → left hook head

The Pull Counter (Tyson's KO weapon): Stand at jab range → bait the jab → shift weight to rear foot → jab misses → EXPLODE with rear hand counter. Pure timing. Practice 50x per session until reflex.

Drill: String at nose height. Bob under 100 times. Every rise = left hook. Do this every session.
INFIGHTING & BODY ATTACK ▼
Getting Inside: Don't walk in — SLIP IN · Bob under their jab, rise inside range · Once inside: tight elbows protect your body · Exit by stepping to an angle

Body-Head System: The body is the tree. Chop the tree → branches fall → head exposed.
1. Left hook body → Right uppercut chin
2. Right hook liver → Left hook temple
3. Left body → Right body → Left hook head (Tyson's pattern)
4. Double left hook: body then head in one motion

The Liver Shot: Left hook to RIGHT side of their torso · Even light liver shots cause involuntary drop · Dig UNDER the elbow · Setup: jab head → guard rises → left hook body
PEEKABOO COMBINATIONS A–Z ▼
Level 1 — Foundation (Month 1–2):
1. Left hook body → Left hook head
2. Right hook body → Left hook head
3. Left hook → Right cross
4. Slip right → Right cross counter

Level 2 — Integration (Month 3–4):
5. Bob left → left hook → right uppercut
6. Slip outside → right hand → roll → left hook
7. Double left hook (body-head) → right cross
8. Pull back → right hand → left hook

Level 3 — Advanced (Month 5+):
9. Jab (Soviet) → slip inside → left hook body → right uppercut
10. Slip right → cross → bob under counter → left hook
11. Right hook body → left hook body → right uppercut
12. HYBRID: Jab range → slip inside → body combo → pivot exit

Learning order: Shadow slow ×50 → Shadow fast ×50 → Bag ×30 → Mitts → Sparring.
PEEKABOO FOOTWORK — CUT OFFS ▼
Cutting the Ring: Don't chase — CUT DIAGONALLY · Block their escape route · They go right → step diagonally right · Herd them to corners and ropes

Step-Step-Punch: Two fast steps close distance → punch lands with step 2 · One moment at range, next inside · Opponent's brain can't process the range change

The fundamental difference: Soviet creates distance — you're the sniper. Peekaboo eliminates it — you're the missile. Both live in you simultaneously.
THE PEEKABOO MENTALITY ▼
"The hero and the coward both feel the same fear. The hero uses it. The coward runs from it." — Cus D'Amato

Mental Rules: You are always the AGGRESSOR · Every opponent miss is YOUR opportunity · Get hit → counter IMMEDIATELY · Head always moving · Pressure is your weapon

Pre-session visualization (2 min every time): Eyes closed. See yourself slipping cleanly. See the counter land perfectly. Your brain cannot distinguish between vivid visualization and reality. This is real training.

🗄 DBMS
ACID Properties: Atomicity (all or nothing), Consistency, Isolation (transactions don't interfere), Durability (committed = permanent)
Normalization: 1NF (atomic values) → 2NF (no partial dependency) → 3NF (no transitive dependency). Know when to denormalize for performance.
Indexing: B-tree index for range queries, hash index for equality. Indexes speed reads, slow writes. Know the trade-off.
SQL Joins: INNER (matching rows) · LEFT (all left + matches) · RIGHT · FULL OUTER. Practice with real queries.
Transactions & Isolation levels: Read Uncommitted → Read Committed → Repeatable Read → Serializable. Know what each prevents.
🌐 COMPUTER NETWORKS
OSI Model: Physical → Data Link → Network → Transport → Session → Presentation → Application. "Please Do Not Throw Sausage Pizza Away"
TCP vs UDP: TCP = reliable, ordered, connection-oriented (HTTP, FTP). UDP = fast, no guarantee (video streaming, DNS, gaming).
HTTP vs HTTPS: HTTPS = HTTP + TLS/SSL encryption. HTTPS uses port 443, HTTP uses 80. Know the TLS handshake basics.
DNS: Domain → IP lookup. Recursive resolver → Root server → TLD server → Authoritative server. Know what caching is and TTL.
⚙️ OPERATING SYSTEMS
Process vs Thread: Process = isolated memory space. Thread = within a process, shared memory. Lighter, faster context switch.
Deadlock conditions (MACH): Mutual exclusion, no preemption, Acquire and hold, Circular wait. All 4 must be present for deadlock.
CPU Scheduling: FCFS, SJF, Round Robin, Priority. Know Round Robin with time quantum — most commonly asked.
Virtual Memory + Paging: Pages mapped to frames. Page fault = page not in RAM, fetch from disk. TLB speeds lookup.
Semaphore vs Mutex: Mutex = binary (1 or 0), used for mutual exclusion. Semaphore = counter, used for signaling.
🏗 SYSTEM DESIGN BASICS
Framework: Clarify requirements → Scale estimation → API design → Data model → Component design → Bottlenecks → Solutions
Load Balancing: Round robin, least connections, IP hash. Prevents single point of failure. Nginx, HAProxy.
Caching: Redis/Memcached. Cache-aside, write-through, write-back patterns. Know cache invalidation strategies.
SQL vs NoSQL: SQL = ACID, relational, structured. NoSQL = scalable, flexible schema (MongoDB, Cassandra, DynamoDB).
Practice designs: URL shortener · Twitter feed · WhatsApp · Netflix. These 4 cover 90% of system design interviews.

🧠 MENTAL PERFORMANCE
5-second rule: When you don't feel like starting — 5-4-3-2-1 and move. Your brain rationalizes laziness in under 5 seconds. Don't give it the chance.
Identity-first habits: Don't say "I'm trying to code." Say "I'm a developer." Don't say "I'm learning boxing." Say "I'm a fighter." Actions follow identity.

Recovery nutrition: Protein within 30 min of training. Carbs to restore glycogen. Creatine daily. Sleep. These four variables account for 80% of your recovery quality.
