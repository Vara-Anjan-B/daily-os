📓 How to Journal

Journaling is not a diary. It's a tool for self-awareness, clarity, and emotional processing. Here's exactly how to do it.

The method — 10 minutes, every night

Sit somewhere quiet. Open a notebook or a notes app. No need for perfect sentences — raw thoughts are fine. The point is to externalize what's in your head so you can look at it clearly. Start with 5 minutes and work up.

Three sections every night:

  1. Gratitude (2 min) — Write 3 specific things you're grateful for today. Not generic ("my family") — specific ("mom called and asked how I was"). This rewires your brain toward positivity over time.
  2. Reflection (5 min) — What happened today? What went well? What felt hard? What do you wish you'd done differently? No judgment — just honest observation.
  3. Intention (3 min) — What is the ONE thing that would make tomorrow a win? Write your top 3 tasks for tomorrow. This moves you from reactive to intentional.

Key Daily Rule

Every rupee counts now
The habit you build tracking ₹50 today is the same habit that tracks ₹50,000 later. Start now.
📋 Daily Finance Checklist
  • Log every expense — even chai and auto fare. Use Google Sheets, Notion, or a notes app. Categories: Food, Transport, Subscription, Misc.
  • Check your UPI/bank balance once — not obsessively, just awareness. Know where you stand daily.
  • Identify one unnecessary spend this week — and cut it next week. One decision per week compounds fast.
  • Save first, spend second — even ₹500/month from now builds discipline. The amount doesn't matter, the habit does.
  • Weekly review every Sunday — total spends vs budget. No guilt, just data. Adjust next week.
  • Learn one finance concept per week — SIP, emergency fund, compound interest, tax basics. YouTube (Ankur Warikoo, CA Rachana) is free.
